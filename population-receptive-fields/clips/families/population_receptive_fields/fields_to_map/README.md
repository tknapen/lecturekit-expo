# fields_to_map: from fitted receptive fields to a map

A manim8 project of the population-receptive-fields deck: 18 s at 1280 by 720 and 30 fps, rendered under the
bridged styles `lecturekit_paper@0.1.0` and `lecturekit_dark@0.1.1` (`lecturekit_dark@0.1.0` before the review lifted its quiet roles). Built from the lecturer's seed
(`animation.yaml`, `references/`, `data/`); the storyboard and the builders are new.

## What the clip shows

Left, the right half of the visual field out to 6.5 deg (semicircle, vertical meridian, fixation cross), labelled
"the visual field: fitted pRF centres". Right, the empty sheet, labelled "a sheet of cortex: one square per voxel".
Below, the key line "colour: distance from fixation".

| Time (s) | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 0.85 | The layout fades in (the style's reveal of every object). |
| 0.85 to 11.6 | The 690 voxels arrive in the order of the `order` column, across the sheet from its left edge to its right, at an even pace: each square and its dot fade in together from 0.3 of their size to full, in the colour of the fitted centre's eccentricity. |
| 11.6 to 13.2 | Rest on the complete eccentricity map. The stop `eccentricity` (label "coloured by eccentricity") is at 12.9 s, frame 387. |
| 13.2 to 15.7 | Every square and dot changes to the colour of the centre's polar angle (2.5 s); positions do not change. The key line cross-fades to "colour: direction from fixation" (13.2 to 14.05). |
| 15.7 to 18 | Hold on the polar-angle map. |

No title and no caption beyond the two labels and the key line.

## How it is built: registered kinds, custom builders, ladder rung

Files: `storyboard.yaml` (written only through the proposals in `proposals/applied/`), `code/prf_map.py` (the two
custom builders, reviewed code), `code/scene_fields_to_map.py` (generated by `scianim codegen`; do not edit).

| Object | Kind | Draws |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `visual_field` | `custom`, `prf_map:visual_field` | semicircle and meridian (role `hairline`), fixation cross (`secondary_text`) |
| `voxels` | `custom`, `prf_map:voxel_fits` (a `ParametricComponent`) | 690 dots in the field and 690 squares on the sheet, from `data/voxels.csv` |
| `field_label`, `sheet_label` | `text_caption` (body, `secondary_text`, top left and top right) | the two labels |
| `key_distance`, `key_direction` | `text_caption` (body, `foreground`, bottom centre) | the key line before and after the change |

| Action | Kind | Window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fits_run` | `vary_parameter` on `voxels.fits`, 0 to 1, linear, no readout | 0.85 to 11.6 s |
| `key_change` | `fade_object` of `key_distance` (out), with `key_direction` revealed at 13.2 s | 13.2 to 14.05 s |
| `recolour_run` | `vary_parameter` on `voxels.recolour`, 0 to 1, the style's easing, no readout | 13.2 to 15.7 s |

Rung reached: R5 (builder objects), with the motion on R4's registered `vary_parameter` (the parametric route). Not
R6, R7 or R8: no `custom_action`, pinned scene or kit scene was needed.

Why the climb from R4 to R5, with the evidence:

- No registered kind draws a half visual field with points placed from a table, or a sheet of squares from a
  table, each coloured on a continuous ramp by a column: `scianim registry` (0.5.0) offers `scientific_plot` and
  `comparison_plot` for tables, both axes plots with one style role per series, and nothing else that takes data.
- The arrival is a stagger across the members of one object. `scianim patterns show reveal_in_reading_order` lists
  it as missing, quoted: "needs: A stagger across the members of one object (the units of a population, the nodes
  of a network), with a lag ratio."
- No action recolours the members of an object by another column.
- The motion is the parametric route, not code that moves things itself: the builder returns a
  `ParametricComponent` with two parameters, `fits` and `recolour`, and the registered `vary_parameter` drives
  them. Without a data binding that names the parameters, validation refuses it, quoted: "action 'fits_run':
  target 'voxels' is a custom; vary_parameter needs one of ['function_image', 'function_plot', 'surface_plot'] or
  a data binding whose transforms name parameters". So the binding declares them as zero-window `smooth`
  transforms of the `order` column (identity at their default 0); the builder refuses any other transform, checks
  that the declaring transforms leave the table unchanged, and never applies a swept value as a window.

The price, in three lines:

- Gives up: nothing of colour, fonts, timing, checkpoints or stops (the builders read the style's palette roles,
  stroke widths, rim and easing, and add no updaters, so checkpoints and the stop are exact); the builder code is
  outside the proposal record and needs the lecturer's review.
- Needs sign-off on: `code/prf_map.py`, the two colour ramps, and the stop (named in `animation.yaml`).
- Way back: none rendered lower. Needs, in manim8's vocabulary: a component that draws table rows as points in a
  visual-field frame; a component that draws table rows as cells of a grid; colour by a data column through a ramp
  of palette roles; a stagger across the members of one object with a lag ratio; an action that changes the colour
  encoding of a data-bound object; declared parameters for custom builders (instead of zero-window transforms).
  When these exist, restage at R4 and compare the same snapshot ids with `scianim compare`.

## Colours

Palette roles only, all present in both bridged styles. Each ramp has three roles ordered in lightness (CIELAB L*)
in both styles, interpolated in CIELAB so the order holds along the ramp; a value beyond the range takes the
nearer end's colour (eccentricities above 6.5 deg take the last colour).

| Colouring | Column, range | Roles | L* paper | L* dark | Least contrast on the page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| distance from fixation | `eccentricity_deg`, 0 to 6.5 | warning, normalization_pool, dn | 39.7, 50.7, 57.2 | 68.7, 73.0, 77.7 | 3.49 (paper), 8.78 (dark) |
| direction from fixation | `polar_angle_deg`, +90 to -90 | dog, comparison, concept | 54.7, 41.5, 37.1 | 80.2, 77.5, 73.5 | 3.79 (paper), 10.19 (dark) |

No ramp passes through the page colour (the soft roles and the background are not used). The field outline and
meridian are `hairline` (contrast 1.75 on paper; 2.6 on black under `lecturekit_dark@0.1.1`, 1.46 under 0.1.0), labels `secondary_text`, the key line
`foreground`, fixation `secondary_text`. Dots take the style's page-coloured rim.

## Data and provenance

`data/voxels.csv`: 690 rows, one per voxel, with `order`, `sheet_u`, `sheet_v`, `x_fit_deg`, `y_fit_deg`,
`sigma_fit_deg`, `eccentricity_deg`, `polar_angle_deg`. Per its citation in `animation.yaml`: a simulation of 690
voxels on a model sheet of early visual cortex, each simulated with noise on a bar run and fitted on its own with
prfmodel; not a measurement. The clip draws `order`, `sheet_u`, `sheet_v`, `x_fit_deg`, `y_fit_deg`,
`eccentricity_deg` and `polar_angle_deg`; `sigma_fit_deg` is not drawn. Geometry and timing follow
`references/original_scene.py` (field centre (-6.3, -0.1) and radius 2.8 for 6.5 deg, sheet height 5.6, squares at
0.94 of the 0.085 step, dot radius 0.05, lag_ratio 0.012, a 2.5 s recolouring), mapped onto the frame with a zoom of
1.12 and an offset so the drawing fills the room between the labels and the key line.

The clip says "a sheet of cortex: one square per voxel" but not that the voxels are simulated (no captions in a
clip): constraint `simulation_label` asks the slide or page to say so.

## Checked

- Validation: `scianim validate`, 0 errors, 2 warnings (the linear pace of `fits_run`, kept because an even pace of
  fitting is what the timeline asks; `linked_views` is named although the scene stages it with a custom builder).
- Final renders, both styles: 1280 by 720, 30 fps, 18.000 s, 540 frames each; the stop at frame 387 with 39 still
  frames before and 9 after (manifest); frame 387 is a keyframe and equals the job's own still. lecturekit measures
  the rests on the pixels: 0.4.0 found 0 frames of unchanged picture before the stop in both styles (below); 0.4.1
  finds 47 (paper) and 46 (dark), and the deck holds it.
- Against the table, on the lossless checkpoints of both styles: 690 occupied cells on the sheet, exactly the
  table's cells; every square's colour matches its row's ramp colour (CIELAB difference at most 0.9); the colour at
  each of 489 unambiguous dot centres matches its voxel (at most 1.6); isolated dots sit within 0.1 px of their
  table position on average. In the movie, at frames 75 and 186 the voxels at full size are exactly ranks 0 to 35
  and 0 to 301 and none from rank 119 and 385 on has appeared.
- Paper and dark draw the same geometry (the drawing's pixel masks agree to an intersection over union of 0.993; the
  text differs by the styles' faces).

## For the lecturer

- Frame 0 is empty: the style reveals every object with a 0.85 s fade from the scene start, so the hard milestone
  `layout` (0 s) is reached at 0.85 s. A deck that starts this clip on a key shows frame 0 as its poster.
  This misses a hard milestone, and manim8 bad3ae1 cannot meet it: every object gets a reveal from alpha 0 at the scene start (`src/scianim/codegen/generate.py`: `begins = scene.start_seconds if obj.reveal_at_seconds is None ...`) and `motion.reveal_seconds` must be greater than 0 (`scianim_core/style.py`: `reveal_seconds: float = Field(gt=0)`). It is a deviation that waits on the lecturer's decision (the deck's README, log). The 0.85 s stays here: the key line's cross-fade at 13.2 s is a reveal and a fade of the
  same length.
- The ramps differ from the original's (three roles instead of four; the polar-angle ramp ends in blue, not teal),
  because no four roles are ordered in lightness in both styles. They borrow the deck's `dn` and `dog` tokens.
- Ten fitted centres lie beyond 6.5 deg and twenty just left of the meridian; they are drawn where the table puts
  them, outside the semicircle.
- The outline and meridian are `hairline`, not `grid` as `direction.prefer` asks: `grid` all but vanishes on black.
- The labels and key line are the style's body text at the safe area's anchors, about 10 % smaller than the
  original's relative to the frame, and the key line is centred rather than a little left of centre.
- `scianim inspect` on the snapshot jobs reports `key_distance` missing at `polar_angle` and `direction_map`: it is
  faded out on purpose by `key_change`; the inspector does not read `fade_object` (on the video jobs it reports
  nothing).
- The stop was not holdable in lecturekit 0.4.0 (found at integration, 13 November 2026; holdable from 0.4.1, whose
  rest measure allows 16 levels, see the deck's README, log). The picture is still from
  11.6 s (manifest: 39 frames of rest), but frame 387 starts a new section, so Manim encodes it as a fresh H.264
  keyframe (CRF 23), and on this picture of 1380 small coloured marks the re-coding differs from the frames before
  it by up to 8 grey levels (paper) and 7 (dark) at lecturekit's measuring size (160 px wide), against its tolerance
  of 6. `lk clips` therefore records `rest_before: 0`, `lk build` refuses `stops="eccentricity"` and
  `tools/check_clips.py` reports the stop. Retiming within the timeline's tolerance does not cure it: a trial with
  the recolouring moved to 13.6 s and to 13.7 s (24 still frames after the stop) passed in paper (6) and failed in
  dark (7). The fix was the engine's: lecturekit 0.4.1 tolerates a keyframe's re-coding, and slide 5 holds the stop.
- No revision has been created; nothing is approved.
